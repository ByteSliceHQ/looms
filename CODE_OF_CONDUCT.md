# Looms Code of Conduct

## Everyone is welcome

Looms welcomes people of all backgrounds, identities, abilities, nationalities, ages, and levels of experience. You can contribute through code, documentation, questions, examples, or reviews. Being new to open source, speaking English as an additional language, or needing an accommodation should not prevent you from participating.

We value open discussion and respectful disagreement. Participation does not require sharing the maintainers' politics or personal beliefs. We judge conduct in the community and contributions to the project fairly, regardless of someone's status, employer, or past contributions.

## Working together

Discuss the work with care for the person doing it. Give specific feedback, explain your reasoning, and leave room for questions and mistakes. Accept corrections and adjust when someone tells you your behavior is causing harm.

Strong technical criticism, disagreement with a maintainer, or a good-faith mistake is not by itself a conduct violation. You may challenge an idea or decision without attacking the person behind it. Keep project discussions relevant to Looms and give others room to participate.

Behavior that prevents people from participating safely is not acceptable. This includes:

- Threats, intimidation, stalking, or harassment
- Discriminatory abuse, slurs, or demeaning attacks on a person's identity or background
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexual harassment
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Repeated personal attacks, targeted humiliation, or deliberate disruption of discussions
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern, helping with a report, or appealing a decision

These expectations apply to contributors and maintainers alike.

## Where this applies

This policy covers Looms repositories, issues, pull requests, project-managed discussion spaces, and project events. It also applies when someone acts as an official representative of Looms. Project-related private messages can be included in a report when they involve harassment or retaliation.

We do not use this policy to police unrelated personal expression or require ideological agreement.

## Report a concern

Email [security@swirls.ai](mailto:security@swirls.ai) with the subject **Looms community conduct**. Share what happened, relevant links or screenshots if available, and any immediate safety concerns. You may report something that affected you or that you witnessed. You do not need to confront the person involved first or prove a case before asking for help.

Tell us if a maintainer is involved or has a conflict of interest. Good-faith reports are welcome even if review finds that no violation occurred.

We will acknowledge your report and explain the next steps. We will handle information discreetly and share it only with people who need it to review or address the concern. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality; where possible, we will discuss necessary disclosures with you first.

## Fair review and response

Maintainers are responsible for applying this policy consistently. Someone directly involved in a complaint must not decide its outcome. Where a conflict leaves no uninvolved maintainer available, we will seek an independent reviewer before making a final decision. Immediate protective steps may still be necessary.

Reviewers will consider the available evidence, context, impact, and any pattern of behavior. When safe and practical, they will give the people involved an opportunity to explain what happened. They will not require a reporter to debate or mediate with the person they reported.

Responses should be proportionate. They may include a request to change behavior, a private warning, removal of harmful content, a temporary participation restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious harm or repeated misconduct may require immediate restrictions; a sequence of warnings is not guaranteed. When a correction is enough, we prefer to help people return to constructive participation.

We will explain decisions and the scope and duration of any restrictions to the affected people, while protecting private information. Reporter identity, seniority, popularity, and financial contributions do not determine the outcome.

## Request a review

If you believe a decision was mistaken or disproportionate, reply to [security@swirls.ai](mailto:security@swirls.ai) with the subject **Looms conduct appeal**. Explain what should be reconsidered and include any relevant context or new information.

We will seek a reviewer who was not involved in the original decision, explain any limits on independent review, and communicate the result. Restrictions remain in effect during an appeal unless maintainers explicitly change them. Raising a concern or appealing in good faith must not result in retaliation.
